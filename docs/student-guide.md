# Student guide: attendance with QR code and face check

## First time (about 3 minutes)

1. Open the attendance site on your own phone and log in with the roll number and
   temporary password from your faculty or the admin.
2. Choose your own password: at least 8 characters, with a letter and a number. Do not
   use your roll number. Never share it: someone who knows it can check in as you.
3. Register your face: press "Start camera" and allow the camera. Take 3 photos when
   asked. Use good light, face the light, hold the phone at eye level, only you in the
   picture. Remove a mask or dark glasses.

Tick "Keep me logged in" on your own phone (not on shared computers), so you do not have
to log in again in class.

## In every class

1. When the faculty shows the QR code, scan it: either "Scan attendance code" on your home
   page, or your phone's normal camera app (tap the link it shows).
2. Press "Start camera", then "Check my face". Look straight, then turn your head slightly
   to the side shown by the arrow, then to the other side. It takes about 5 seconds.
3. "You are marked present" means you are done. You have 60 seconds after scanning.

## If something goes wrong

- "Code expired": scan the code that is on the screen now.
- "Time ran out": scan again.
- "We could not see your head turn": follow the arrows and turn a little more.
- "Your face did not match": move into better light. After 3 failed tries, tell your
  faculty; they can mark you present.
- Camera blocked: tap the lock icon next to the address, allow Camera, reload the page.
- "Not your course": you are not enrolled; tell your faculty.

Your attendance per course is on your home page. If a class looks wrong, talk to your
faculty; they can correct it.

Photos are not stored. Only a numeric description of your face is kept, to check that it
is you.
