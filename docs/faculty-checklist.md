# Faculty checklist: taking attendance

## Before class (5 minutes)

1. Laptop charged or plugged in, connected to the internet.
2. Open the attendance site at its usual https address and log in.
3. Open your course. Check "Attendance is running" is NOT shown from an earlier class.
4. Connect the projector. Close other windows so the code is not covered.

## Start of class

1. Press "Start attendance". The QR code appears and changes every 10 seconds.
2. Press "Full screen" so students at the back can scan. Dim lights near the screen if
   the code has glare.
3. Tell students: scan the code, press "Start camera", then follow the arrows (look
   straight, turn your head slightly each way).
4. Watch "present" go up. The latest names appear under the code.

If the code goes blurry with "Code out of date", the laptop lost its connection; it comes
back by itself when the connection returns. Check the Wi-Fi.

## Students who cannot check in

- "Not your course": they are not enrolled. Ask the admin to enrol them; mark them by hand.
- "Register your face": they never finished registration. They can do it now on their phone.
- Face did not match 3 times, camera broken, or cannot turn their head: on the attendance
  screen, scroll to the student list, open "Change" next to the student, type a reason
  (for example "face check failed, seen in class") and press "Mark present".
- Forgot their password: on the course page, "Actions" next to the student,
  "New password". Read the temporary password to them; they set their own at login.

## End of class

1. Press "Stop attendance". Everyone who did not check in is recorded as absent.
2. Look at the totals. Fix any mistakes with "Change" and a reason. Every change is
   recorded with your name and the time.

If you forget to press Stop, attendance closes by itself after 2 hours.

## Later

- Class held without the code (projector broken, roll on paper): course page, "Record
  another date". Tick who was present, give a reason.
- Reports: course page, "Download report" (Excel or CSV), or "Show students below 75%".
